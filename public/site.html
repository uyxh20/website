In-Between

Where the Human and the Digital Intersect


I’m Ulysse Ha, a qualitative (ethnographic) researcher trained in Anthropology (MSc) and Sociology (BSc). This website is dedicated to documenting my projects, observations, and thoughts.

It all began with a heuristic in Anthropology: what we create changes who we are. In today's world, how are worldviews and behaviors reshaped by our daily interaction with different technological systems? Throughout my formative academic training, looking at the intersection of human and digital allowed me to understand how behavioral change is induced and sustained by a change in perception of reality. And it will continue to serve as my anchor as I now navigate my professional pursuits.

Taken from Recursivity and Contingency (Yuk Hui, 2019), where I was first exposed to the ideas of recursivity and ontogenesis. Ontogenesis refers to the beginning of an evolving ontology. Within the context of my question, it embodies the process of iteration that is dynamic and recursive in nature.

Bio

Working across Europe and Asia

Select a city to explore the journey.

2011–2015 · Born in Paris and spent my formative lycée years there, before studying sociology, anthropology, and technology across Europe.

2017–2022 · Grew up in Hong Kong and returned for several professional chapters: an urban-planning placement with Cushman & Wakefield, qualitative market research for organisations including Google and Tencent, and the Carlsberg digital graduate programme, supporting ERP and CRM rollouts.

2015–2019 · Completed a BSc in Sociology at the University of Bath, building a foundation in social theory and qualitative research.

2017–2018 · During my placement with Cushman & Wakefield, I researched transit-oriented development across Shanghai, Chengdu, and Shenzhen.

2019–2020 · Completed an MSc in Digital Anthropology at UCL with distinction and Dean’s List recognition, developing an ethnographic approach to understanding how digital systems shape behaviour.

2022–present · Progressed from product management into AI product management, bringing a qualitative-research perspective to how teams define, build, and adopt digital products.
